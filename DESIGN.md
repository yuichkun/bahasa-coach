# Bahasa Coach interface

The user's current direction is a minimal, focused conversation surface. Use a single reading column, a neutral white background, restrained type and a small text navigation bar. No sidebar, hero copy, dashboard metrics, decorative cards, or repeated explanations on the conversation screen.

Voice starts directly with one action. Optional topic exercises belong in a collapsed control. During conversation the visible content is the transcript, up to three short reply examples, and start/end/microphone controls. The primary in-call control is a large central microphone toggle. Keep ending the call separate on the left and the current cost as quiet text on the right. Use a microphone/slashed microphone icon, color, and a short on/off label together; show M as the shortcut. Ignore keyboard shortcuts while editing, composing text, or using dialogs. There is no action menu and no separate thinking-break or resume button. The transcript uses paragraphs, not transport chunks. User text is slightly muted; coach text remains clear and readable. Useful automatic language feedback appears directly below the corresponding learner utterance, with a natural expression and one short Japanese explanation. It never replaces the transcript or pauses the conversation. No transcript-editing controls appear in the conversation UI.

Only words with a ready contextual annotation or reusable dictionary meaning are interactive and have a subtle dotted underline. Unknown words remain plain text with no help cursor, tab stop, or empty/loading popover. Hover, click, and focus only read the local cache; they never initiate a lookup. Prefetch visible and near-visible text proactively, including earlier captions when scrolling back. Start with whole words while speech is arriving, defer partial trailing tokens, and update readiness as soon as results arrive. Prioritize missing definitions over refining known ones. Voice captions pair each original sentence with its Japanese translation directly underneath, visible without hover or click. Word popups contain only word meanings and formal forms. Reply hints keep their existing Japanese text. Do not add a sentence-translation toolbar or menu. Preserve full sentence context and adjacent conversation when requesting translations; translate automatically and cache by both sentence and context. Keep a displayed sentence translation readable while later conversation adds context; invalidate it if its sentence or preceding context changes. Word explanations open in one viewport-clamped portal so the transcript's scrolling container cannot clip them. Keep the reading position while a word is inspected. Retry failed background preparation automatically; do not send the learner into a word-lookup recovery flow.

Every voice lesson ends on a separate, automatically generated lesson notebook route. This reading surface is warm and instructional: cream background, dark olive text, yellow marker highlights for useful contrasts, gentle green highlights for successful expressions, a compact topic index, source/natural-expression comparisons, teacher explanations and applied examples. Cover the full lesson by topic without an arbitrary item or length cap. Preserve already-natural colloquial wording. Anchor every teaching point to actual conversation text; show its surrounding context on request. Do not show scores or unsupported mastery claims. A point can lead into existing focused writing practice.

Microphone off closes the voice connection and stops ongoing voice charges. Microphone on reconnects the same lesson. The icon and M key perform exactly the same transition, in the same place, retaining keyboard focus, captions, and hints. Show a pending state while switching and never report off before closure succeeds. Reconnection initialization pricing belongs in connection settings, not a separate control flow.

Visual verification must not use computer use or browser automation without the user's explicit request. Use source inspection and isolated DOM/unit tests.

The conversation partner is Rani, explicitly identified as fictional in the empty state. Her everyday life supplies topics; responses can be reactions and anecdotes without obligatory questions. Keep her background consistent across sessions and microphone reconnects. Her stories are never learner facts.

Rani shares responsibility for moving the conversation forward. Open with a greeting and one brief current situation, leaving its cause and details for later turns. Completed short learner replies are enough: add one new concrete detail, opinion, or choice on the same topic rather than repeating the last fact or merely acknowledging. Usually use one or two short sentences, then yield; silence remains thinking time. Questions are occasional and tied to the current topic, not broad profile or habit interviews. Supply a situation seed rather than a complete plot or scripted example. Prior coach filler must not become style examples; earlier topics are continuity metadata, not proof that an invented event occurred.

The microphone control silences local playback and capture immediately, retaining the connection only while finalization runs. If closure is unconfirmed, the same control offers retry without resuming sound. Translation settings expose only three native radio choices: speed, balance, and precision. No model identifiers or reasoning jargon appear in the interface. Existing translated captions remain readable when preferences change.
